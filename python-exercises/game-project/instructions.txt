
CASTLE ESCAPE - INSTRUCTIONS

Your goal is to explore the castle, collect useful items and find a way to escape.
Type 1 to enter the dungeon, 2 to enter the courtyard and 3 to enter the tower.

You can explore the areas in different orders and revisit rooms to collect items you may have missed.

Collect the Unlit Torch from the dungeon and the Lighter from the courtyard.
You will need both of them before you can properly explore the tower.

Inside the tower, search your inventory to light the torch.
After completing the dungeon, courtyard and tower, type 4 to enter the final chamber.

COMMANDS:

collect - Pick up the item in your current room.

inventory - View the items in your inventory.

profile - View your name, age, health, level, current location and inventory.

pack - Add an item of your choice to your inventory.

instructions - Show these instructions again.

save - Save your current progress. This replaces the previous save.

load - Load your previously saved progress.

quit - Quit the game.

Your choices can affect your health, level, good and bad choices, and the ending of the game.
Your decisions involving Guts can also change the final ending.

Explore carefully and good luck escaping the castle!
