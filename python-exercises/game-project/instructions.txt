
CASTLE ESCAPE - INSTRUCTIONS

Your goal is to explore the castle, collect useful items and find a way to escape.

Type 1 for the dungeon, 2 for the courtyard and 3 for the tower.

You can explore in different orders and revisit rooms to collect things you missed.
Collect the unlit torch from the dungeon and the lighter from the courtyard.
Search your inventory in the tower to light the torch.
After finishing all three areas, type 4 to enter the final chamber.

collect - pick up the item in your current room
inventory - view your items
profile - view your name, age, health, level and location
pack - add an item of your choice to your inventory
instructions - show these instructions again
save - save your progress, replacing the previous save
load - load your saved progress
quit - quit the game

The map and tower key are extra collectibles.
Food and potions are loot. There isn't a use-item command.
Your choices affect your health, items and the ending.
Helping, ignoring or stealing from the knight leads to a different ending.

Explore carefully and good luck escaping the castle!
